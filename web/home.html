Questions

Good to know.

Does Dairee take payments?

No. Dairee keeps the record: bills, payments, advances and credit. Customers pay the farm the way they always have, and the office notes it down.

Do customers need to install anything?

No. They open your farm's link in any browser and sign in with their number and the password the farm gives them.

What if there is no signal on the round?

Keep ticking. Each tick is kept on the phone and sent as soon as there is a signal again.

Who can see the money?

Only the people you give the To collect page. Delivery staff see their round and nothing else.

Can I get my records out?

Yes. Customers, deliveries, bills, payments, pauses and the herd come out as spreadsheets for Excel or Google Sheets.

Can one person work at more than one farm?

Yes. One sign-in works for every farm you are added to, and you switch between them from your profile.
