Support

Help with Dairee. Email contact@skifi.co and we will reply.

I work at a farm and cannot sign in

Sign in with the WhatsApp number or email your farm added for you. If your farm gave you a temporary password, use Email, then Use a password. Still stuck: ask your farm's office to check your number or email.

I am a customer

Choose "I'm a customer" and sign in with your mobile number and the password your farm gave you. Forgot it? Ask the farm for a new one.

Starting a farm

Sign in, name your farm, set your milk price in Settings, then add your customers and their vessels.
